MBA Lounge — Child Safety Standards

App: MBA Lounge · Developer: Convum Partners · Last updated: 2026-08-08

MBA Lounge (developer: Convum Partners) prioritizes child safety and strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This document sets out MBA Lounge's child safety standards in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Scope and Nature of Service

MBA Lounge is an adults-only community service for MBA students and alumni aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children (under 18) and does not permit minors to register or use the service.

2. Prohibition of CSAE / CSAM

The service prohibits all content and conduct related to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Any violating content is removed immediately, the responsible account is suspended, and the matter is reported to the relevant authorities as required by law.

3. Reporting and Response

Users can report child-safety concerns via the in-app content reporting feature or the contact below. Reports are reviewed promptly and appropriate action is taken, including content removal, account action, and reporting to authorities.

4. Child Safety Point of Contact

Report or inquire about child safety issues: 728haven@gmail.com
Contact: MBA Lounge Operations Team (Convum Partners)

5. Compliance

MBA Lounge complies with applicable laws of the Republic of Korea and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, and maintains these standards prohibiting child sexual abuse and exploitation on an ongoing basis.
